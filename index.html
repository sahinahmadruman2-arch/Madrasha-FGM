THE NAME

Friends Group Madrasha.

FGM stands for Friends Group Madrasha. FGM Era is an unofficial community website created just for our circle of friends. It brings our member list, fun rankings, notices and memories together in one place.

This is not the official website of any madrasha or educational institution. It is an independent friends project, made to celebrate our group.
